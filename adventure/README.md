# Mooshak’s Modak Mission — Grand Adventure

An expanded HTML5 Canvas adventure with three levels: Marigold Streets, Temple Garden, and Grand Pandal. Open index.html or serve this folder with any static web server. No build, framework, or installation is needed.

## Controls
- Arrow keys / A and D: move
- Space / W / Up: jump; press again to double jump
- E or USE: interact with the nearby puzzle gate
- P / Escape: pause
- Onscreen buttons support touch controls.
- Sound starts off. Enable it with the sound button for synthesized music and effects.

## Goal and additions
Each level requires at least 8 of 10 modaks, all 3 flowers, and its puzzle before the exit opens. Finish all three levels and choose one pandal decoration after each: garland, lighting, and rangoli. Each decoration uses the level's offering requirement as its unlock cost; result counts continue to show what was collected.

- A 12-second magnet attracts nearby modaks.
- Blessings protect for 8 seconds, including rescuing falls.
- Checkpoints restore your position after damage, keeping collected offerings. Three lost hearts require a level retry; earlier levels and decorations are preserved.
- Puzzles: four-lamp memory sequence, an X-shaped rangoli, and four consecutive timed drum taps. The drum puzzle includes a slower option.
- Stars per level: one for completion, one for every offering, one for no lost hearts. Up to 9 stars total. Timing excludes pauses, puzzles, and decoration screens.
- Mooshak has animated ears, feet, and tail, plus a victory dance. Reduced-motion settings simplify animation.

The original submitted game remains at the repository root. This expanded version lives in /adventure/.

Built with ChatGPT/Codex assistance. Festival background artwork was AI-generated; the mouse is drawn with Canvas. Music and effects are synthesized with Web Audio. Emoji appearance varies by platform.

## Clear level order and completion goodies
Press Play, read the Level 1 goal, and press Play Level 1. Finish the level and place a pandal decoration to unlock the Level 2 introduction; repeat for Level 3. The progress strip marks completed, current, and locked levels. Introductions do not run the timer.

Completing all three levels unlocks the Festival Hero trophy plus Golden Crown and Lotus Scarf outfits. Open your goodies on the celebration screen, choose an outfit, and replay wearing it. My goodies on the title screen opens rewards earned on an earlier visit. Reward unlocks and outfit choice are saved locally in the same browser; if storage is unavailable, rewards work for the current visit. These are cosmetic rewards and do not change physics or scoring.
